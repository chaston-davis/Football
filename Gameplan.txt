Features:

- Pregame Random Introduction for each team
- Stadium Description for each team
- 32 Teams

- Running Back (Can catch and run)

- Center (Can Snap the Ball, and block)
- Rest of Offensive Line (Can Block)
- Defense (Can Catch, and Tackle)
- Kicker (Can kick, catch, throw, and run)
- Long Snapper (Can catch)
- Weather and Temperature
